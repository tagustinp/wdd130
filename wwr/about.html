White Water Rafting

White Water Rafting organizes trips, provides the watercraft, life jackets, and everything you need for a thrilling and safe experience for you, your friends and your family with experienced guides

History

During the spring of 1962 Abraham Lincoln placed a small raft into the Stanislaus River and ventured into the unknown. He lived to tell about it and fell in love with rivers and rafting. Soon he was sharing his new found love with others. A lot has been learned since those early days of rafting, but the same joy and excitement initially experienced on the river has only grown. And so has the White Water Rafting family. Now, over 60 years later, three generations of Lincolns and the White Water Rafting staff welcome more than 10,000 guests on river trips each year!
